What does Eventeny's Event Score Mean

 

Your Event Score on Eventeny is a number out of 100 that reflects the completeness, engagement, and activity of your event listing. It directly influences how high your event ranks in search results, both on the homepage and in time/location-based searches on the all events page. 
 

In short, the higher your score, the more people will see your event. Think of it as your event’s visibility grade. The best part is that you have full control to improve it!

 

 

How Your Event Score Is Calculated

 

Your Event Score is based on three main areas: event content, company details, and account health. Each category reflects how complete, engaging, and active your event presence is on Eventeny. The more optimized your event and account, the higher your score and visibility.

 

 

Event Content (65%)

 

This is the single most important factor in your score. It measures how robust, interactive, and timely your event listing is.

 

Here’s how to max it out:

  • Timing matters: The closer your event date, the more urgency it creates, and the higher your score climbs. However, publishing your event early also gives you a bonus, so the best strategy is to publish as early as possible.
     
  • Participant engagement: Events with multiple ways to engage rank higher.  Add ticketing, applications, maps, schedules, and other modules to give attendees and vendors more ways to participate.
     
  • Rich event details: A detailed listing performs better. Make sure to include:
     
    • A description that’s at least 500 characters.
    • Three or more images that bring your event to life.
    • Terms, policies, and parking info, often overlooked, but great for bonus points and building trust.
       
  • Traffic & interactions: Once your event is live, engagement helps boost your score, page views, clicks, and shares all count. Promote your Eventeny page on social, email, or your website to grow traffic and improve visibility.
     

 

Company Details (25%)

 

This part of your score looks at your overall presence and activity on Eventeny.

 

Ways to improve:

  • Host more events: The more events you host on Eventeny, the stronger your profile becomes. Consistency pays off; ensure that all your events, including smaller ones, are listed year-round!
     
  • Grow your team: Eventeny is built for collaboration. Add team members to your account, from volunteers to staff, to unlock planning tools and boost your score.
     

  • Stay consistent: Accounts with history and regular usage naturally score higher over time. Loyalty and repeat use are rewarded.

     

 

Account Health (10%)

 

Think of this as your Eventeny wellness check.

 

Boost this area by:

  • Subscribing to a paid plan: Paid plans (Starter, Plus, and Pro) automatically carry more weight in your score calculation. They also unlock advanced tools to help your event and team grow!
     
  • Logging in regularly: Accounts with recent activity (such as logging in, updating content, or responding to applicants) are considered active and reliable, and are rewarded accordingly.
